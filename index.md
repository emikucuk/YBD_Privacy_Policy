# Privacy Policy for yinebeyindurdu Automation

**Last updated: August 20, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how our automation tool collects, uses, and protects information when integrating with Instagram (Meta Graph API) and YouTube (YouTube Data API v3) to distribute short-form content.

## 1. Information We Access and Collect
To automate content publishing, this application requires access to certain API services. We only access the minimum data necessary to function:
*   **YouTube Data API v3:** We use this service to upload short-form videos to your YouTube channel. We access OAuth 2.0 authentication tokens and basic channel metadata required for publishing.
*   **Meta Graph API:** We use this service to publish content to your Instagram account. We access authorization tokens and basic profile information necessary for content distribution.

## 2. How We Use the Information
The data accessed is used strictly for:
*   Automating the distribution of short-form video content to connected social media platforms.
*   Authenticating API requests to Meta and Google servers on your behalf.

We do not collect personal data from viewers. We do **not** sell, rent, or share any data, including API tokens or account information, with any third parties.

## 3. Third-Party Policies and Revocation
By using this automation, you are also bound by the terms and privacy policies of the third-party platforms we integrate with. You have the right to revoke our application's access to your accounts at any time.

*   **Google & YouTube:** Our application uses YouTube API Services. By using our service, you agree to be bound by the [YouTube Terms of Service](https://www.youtube.com/t/terms) and the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). You can revoke access to your data at any time via the [Google Security Settings page](https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions).
*   **Instagram & Meta:** By connecting your Instagram account, you agree to Meta's [Privacy Policy](https://privacycenter.instagram.com/policy). You can manage or revoke access in your Instagram or Meta Business account settings.

## 4. Data Storage and Security
OAuth credentials and API tokens are stored securely locally and are used exclusively for programmatic publishing. We do not retain these tokens if access is revoked.
